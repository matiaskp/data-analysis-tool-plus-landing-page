# Product knowledge: ClientBridge

Facts only. Everything here is live on clientbridge-crm.com today. If a
prospect asks for something not listed, say you'll check, never promise it.

## What it is

A CRM made in Paraguay for small businesses that sell over WhatsApp. One
workspace for the whole team: WhatsApp chats, contacts, deals, tasks,
invoices and products. English and Spanish. Website: clientbridge-crm.com.
Live demo without signing up: clientbridge-crm.com/demo (or /es).

## What it does (live)

- **WhatsApp inside the CRM.** The business connects its own WhatsApp number
  by scanning a QR code (like WhatsApp Web). Text, photos and files both ways.
  The team answers from the computer; each chat is linked to a contact.
- **No chat gets lost.** Every customer who writes is saved with their name
  and history. The inbox filters Unread and "Needs a human".
- **Pipeline.** Deals move through stages (drag and drop; tabs on the phone).
  Tasks, notes, calendar.
- **Invoices.** Sale and purchase invoices with line items and PDF download.
  Overdue invoices are flagged. Creating an invoice adjusts stock automatically.
- **Products.** Price, cost, stock, photos, vendor, category. A rep can drop a
  product into a chat with a shortcut.
- **Team.** Roles: Admin, Manager, Sales Rep, Vendor, View-Only, with
  departments. Audit log. Bulk import of contacts from Excel/CSV (Starter+).
- **Message templates and auto-reply bot (Business plan).** Templates that fill
  in the customer's name, business hours, catalog. A menu bot answers new
  contacts (hours, prices, catalog, location), up to 3 menu levels, and hands
  the chat to a person when asked, marking it "Needs a human". It only replies
  to people who write first; it never sends bulk messages.
- **Dashboard.** "Needs you": chats waiting, unread messages, overdue tasks
  and invoices. Revenue analytics on Business.

## Buying triggers

They run Meta/Instagram ads to WhatsApp, have several people answering one
number, just hired a salesperson or receptionist, opened a second branch, or
sell online (Tiendanube, Shopify, WooCommerce) and answer questions on WhatsApp.
