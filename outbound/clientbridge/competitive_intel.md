# Competitive intel: ClientBridge vs WhatsApp CRMs in Latin America

Use these to answer "ya uso X" or "¿en qué se diferencia de X?". Never run a
competitor down, never quote their prices as fact (they change; say
"según su web" if you mention one). Lead with what's different, not with
what's wrong with them.

## Where ClientBridge is different (true for all of them)

- **Made in Paraguay**, Spanish with real local support from the founder.
- **The whole back office, not just the inbox:** contacts → deals → invoices
  with PDF → product stock, in one place. Most WhatsApp tools stop at the chat
  and the pipeline.
- **Per user, not per conversation or message.** Costs don't grow when chats do.
- **Simple:** connect the number with a QR, no Meta business verification needed.

## Kommo (formerly amoCRM)

Big, mature, multi-channel (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok), strong automation
and "AI" marketing, many local partners/agencies. Usually needs a partner to
set up. Angle: "Si Kommo te resulta mucho para lo que necesitas, ClientBridge
es más simple y además factura y lleva el stock."

## Leadsales

Mexican conversational CRM for SMBs, WhatsApp + social, pipeline-focused.
Angle: invoices and stock included; local support in Paraguay.

## Whaticket

Multi-agent WhatsApp inbox plus CRM and social channels. Angle: ClientBridge
goes past the inbox: deals, invoices, products in the same place.

## Cliengo

Argentine chatbot/AI agent platform with a free plan, strong on web chat and
Meta Ads leads. Angle: ClientBridge is where the sale continues after the
bot: contact, deal, invoice.

## Mercately

WhatsApp CRM for LatAm with e-commerce integrations. Angle: invoices with
stock in the same tool, priced per user.

## Wati, ChatDaddy, Callbell, Treble, Botmaker

Mostly built around the official WhatsApp API and broadcasts, priced with
message costs on top. Angle: no per-message costs, no Meta verification,
working in an afternoon. Be honest if they need bulk campaigns: ClientBridge
doesn't do broadcasts.

## HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive

General CRMs where WhatsApp is an add-on or integration, English-first
pricing in USD with higher tiers for automation. Angle: WhatsApp is the
center of ClientBridge, not a plug-in.

## WhatsApp Business app on its own

The real incumbent for most prospects. Angle: same number, but the history
is saved per customer and the whole team sees it, plus deals and invoices.
